Major NPCs of Zaharât: A GM's Guide

Molochia Vadhat
Herald of the Tree, Founder, Leader of Taqlidi
First Impression:
A desert elf in a wheelchair, dressed in traditional Jaang caravan garb, layered flowing silks in sun-bleached colors, intricate silver jewelry, indigo-dyed headwrap. She looks exactly like what a tourist expects a "wise desert elder" to look like. Too exactly.
Physical Description:

Age appearance: Ageless in that unsettling elf way, but something's off
Hair: Long, decorated with beads and charms from a dozen cultures, you notice different ones each time you look
Eyes: Deep purple, or were they green? Did they just shift color mid-conversation, or are you imagining it?
Hands: Delicate, covered in henna patterns that seem to change motifs between meetings
The wheelchair: Ornate brass and wood, covered in carved symbols. Some are historical Jaang script. Others are... something else. The wheels sometimes leave faint afterimages
Condition: Weakens visibly when leaving the city limits. Near the Tree, she seems almost vibrant. Away from it, she fades

... Have you really saw like purple tendr-... roots from her dress?

The Wrongness:
You can never quite pinpoint what's wrong with her. It's different every time:

One moment her breathing seems too slow, like she's operating at 0.5x speed
Next meeting, she blinks at odd intervals, three times rapid, then not at all for minutes
Sometimes she responds to questions you haven't asked yet
Other times she answers yesterday's conversation in today's meeting
She uses idioms from languages that don't exist yet
Her shadow occasionally moves independently, just slightly out of sync
When she turns her head, there's a delay before her jewelry catches up
Tea she poured an hour ago is still steaming

Temperament:
The Sweetest Monster
With children and loved ones, she's a perfect fairy-tale grandmother:

Makes hand-knitted scarves for friends while discussing philosophy
Donates fortunes to orphanages and personally teaches reading to street kids
Explains complex morality with patience that spans centuries
Remembers everyone's birthdays, their children's names, their favorite tea blend

But her moral framework is alien:

Suggests cannibalism of the recently dead with the same cheerful practicality as making sandwiches
Will casually mention "waiting forty years for this conversation to ripen"
Takes things without asking, artifacts, resources, people, but gives them back cheerfully if you request it
Discusses mass death with the emotional weight of discussing traffic delays
Loves deeply and genuinely, but in ways that sometimes destroy what she loves

Dialogue Patterns:

Speaks in long, meandering stories that somehow answer your question three topics later
Uses "recently" to mean anything from yesterday to five decades ago
Asks "how are your grandchildren?" to people who don't have children yet
Mixes tenses freely: "I will have been considering this for thirty years now"
Never interrupts, because she has infinite patience. Literally.

Body Language:

Stillness that goes beyond calm, she can sit motionless for unsettling lengths
When she does move, it's fluid and deliberate
Touches objects gently, reverently, as if everything is infinitely fragile
Smiles with genuine warmth that somehow makes you more uncomfortable
Makes eye contact that lasts just slightly too long

Earning Her Curiosity:
Show her something genuinely new, nearly impossible for someone who's seen the development of cultures across centuries. Novel combinations, unexpected applications, true creativity. She'll study you like a beloved experiment.
Earning Her Wrath:
Threaten the Tree. Threaten the archives. Threaten the long-term future of knowledge preservation. She won't rage, she'll simply fix the problem with the efficiency of someone moving chess pieces. Your city doesn't burn; it's gently, lovingly, irreversibly transformed into something that can never threaten her again.
GM Notes:

She's never lying, but she experiences time non-linearly, so her truths are temporally complex
She genuinely loves people but doesn't experience urgency the way mortals do
Three hundred years in the Plane of Time didn't make her immortal; it made her atemportal
The wheelchair it's an anchor to physical reality
She collects hobbies across decades: embroidery, pottery, seventeen languages, taxidermy, opera composition
Has absolutely called her husband "dear" in a conversation that won't happen for five years


Walì Vadhat
Bearer of Progress, Founder, Leader of Hukuma
First Impression:
A grumpy desert elf who clearly used to be something more. The mood's like those "old shopkeeper who was a legendary adventurer". Looks like he woke up with a hangover, got dressed in yesterday's clothes, and decided that was good enough for ruling a city-state.
Physical Description:

Age appearance: Actually looks his age, ancient for an elf, with visible exhaustion in every line
Build: Gone to seed. Broad shoulders now softened, the build of someone who was once extremely fit but hasn't exercised in decades except lifting bottles
Hair: Completerly bald. Yes he is a pureblooded elf. No he *did* have them. No they won't regrow. "An old incident after an old party".
Face: Weathered, with the permanent squint of someone who's seen too much. Stubble that suggests he forgot to shave. Again.
Clothing: Practical desert wear, loose trousers, worn leather vest, boots that have seen a thousand miles. Everything is functional but rumpled, like he got dressed in the dark
Eyes: Sharp grey-green, the only part of him that's still clearly dangerous. They track everything with predatory awareness hidden behind apparent disinterest
The Flask: Always visible somewhere on his person. Well-worn leather, Jaang designs, never quite empty
Hands: Scarred, steady despite the drinking, with the calluses of both a swordsman and an alchemist

The Tiredness:
Not physical exhaustion, existential weariness:

Sighs before speaking, like every conversation costs him years
Moves with efficiency that's become habit, no wasted motion
Sits heavily, like gravity affects him more than others
Rubs his temples when people are being stupid (constantly)
Drinks not to get drunk, but to maintain a specific level of numbness

Temperament:
The Exhausted Idealist Turned Pragmatic Cynic
His default state is "leave me alone":

Responds to most requests with variations of "figure it out yourself"
Attends exactly zero social functions unless forced
Makes cutting observations about everyone's stupidity
Treats most problems with indifference bordering on contempt

But crack the shell:

Sharp-tongued blasphemer: Will launch into rants about divine incompetence, political stupidity, and the general inadequacy of existence. Viciously funny when angry. Uses profanity like an art form.
Empathetic friend: Capable of profound understanding about loss, failure, compromise, and the weight of surviving when others didn't. Will share a bottle and your pain with equal generosity.
Strategic genius: The indifference is performance. He's tracking seventeen problems simultaneously and has contingencies for each.

Dialogue Patterns:

Short, blunt sentences punctuated by "fuck," "shit," and creative blasphemy
Interrupts long explanations with "get to the point"
Makes cutting observations that expose exactly what you're hiding
Uses dark humor as a defense mechanism
When he actually cares, speaks in careful, precise language that reveals depths of thought
Occasionally slips into discussing the old days, then catches himself and goes quiet

Body Language:

Slouches in chairs like they offended him personally
Gestures minimally, when he does move, it's economical and precise
Drinks with practiced casualness (he's maintaining, not descending)
Makes eye contact rarely, but when he does, it's piercing
Rubs his wedding band unconsciously when thinking about Molochia
Tenses at mentions of gods, planes, or meteors, ancient reflexes die hard

Earning His Curiosity:
Do something that contradicts his understanding of human nature. Show genuine selflessness in a system built on self-interest. Solve a problem he thought unsolvable. Surprise him with genuine innovation. Most importantly: be interesting after he's seen everything. He'll chase you across continents just to understand why you're different.
Earning His Wrath:
Mess with planar entities. Attempt to create monopolies on essential goods. Threaten the city's foundations. Experiment with powers you don't understand. He won't chase you, he'll arrange for the problem to solve itself via spontaneous combustion, market forces, or mysterious disappearance. If he does come for you personally, you've done something that reminds him of old horrors.
GM Notes:

The drinking isn't weakness, it's self-medication for trauma and temporal dysphoria
He's playing 4D chess while pretending not to play at all
Genuinely loves Molochia but is slowly losing the ability to connect with her across their temporal divide
Has contingencies for everything, including his own death
The indifference is armor; underneath is someone who cares so much it hurts
Will absolutely threaten nuclear options while sounding bored about it
His tired cynicism masks the fact that he's still trying after two centuries


Lixander "Vadhat"
Founder of Amal Dua'i, The Ambitious Nephew
First Impression:
An enormous drake (think: 12-foot crocodilian dragon) stuffed into an immaculate white-collar business suit, carrying a briefcase, speaking with the corporate professionalism of a loan officer who moonlights as a folk devil.
Physical Description:

Species: Drake, Descended from Franco, the Vadhat couple's former riding mount who was uplifted by Molochia
Size: Large enough to be impressive, not so large he can't fit through doors (barely)
Scales: Deep bronze-green with iridescent highlights, meticulously groomed
Eyes: Golden with slit pupils, constantly calculating
The Suit: Bespoke, white collar, somehow never rumpled despite his size. The tie is always perfectly knotted. He has a collection in various fabrics, each subtly wrong for formal occasions.
Accessories: Leather briefcase (reinforced), reading glasses (pure affectation, he has perfect vision), pocket watch (inherited from Franco)
The Tattoo: A glowing marking resembling Madame Luna's symbol, visible on his neck/shoulder. Sometimes pulses with faint light. He usually covers it with high collars, but it shows through when he's agitated or making deals.
Posture: Upright, professional, completely at odds with the predatory physiology

The Wrongness:
He's simultaneously:

A legendary dragon from ancient folklore
A middle-manager filling out expense reports
The trickster devil from every cultural cautionary tale
Someone genuinely concerned about worker's rights

Temperament:
The Hypocritical Bastard With A Conscience Who Hates Hypocritical Bastards With Consciences
He's brutally self-aware about his contradictions:

Genuinely wants to help the disenfranchised
Also absolutely using them as a political power base
Hates systemic injustice
Also profits from it extensively
Advocates for the living
Also makes deals with outer-planar entities for personal gain
Despises the Vadhat family's control
Also trades on the name constantly when useful

Dialogue Patterns:

Shifts between corporate-speak and direct threats seamlessly
"As I was saying about the quarterly workforce satisfaction metrics, work with me or I'll fucking eat you, now, regarding the pension proposals..."
Thinks out loud, sometimes revealing strategies he shouldn't
Caustic wit, especially about hypocrisy (including his own)
Makes deals with everyone: "I tried to kill you last month, but that was business. This is different business."
Mixes high philosophy with crude pragmatism

The Deal-Making:
When shaking hands with Lixander:

The air feels thicker
Shadows lengthen slightly
You hear distant bells/drums/whispers from your culture's folklore
His eyes reflect light wrong for just a moment
You feel like you've just agreed to something you didn't quite understand
(You probably have)

Body Language:

Predatory stillness when listening, he's a dragon pretending to be civilized
Precise, economical movements with his clawed hands
Tail movements betray his mood (twitching when annoyed, still when calculating)
Adjusts his tie when uncomfortable
Looks at people like he's calculating their value in multiple currencies simultaneously
Smiles with too many teeth, the corporate smile of something that could absolutely eat you

The Vadhat Name Game:

In Zaharât: "Yes, I'm that Vadhat" (uses it for authority)
In foreign nations: "Just Lixander, humble art dealer" (hides it to avoid prejudice)
When making deals: Flashes it like a credit card
When threatened: "You know who my family is, right?"
In his head: Resents every second he needs it

Earning His Alliance:
Be useful. Be interesting. Be honest about your self-interest. He respects naked ambition more than false altruism. Acknowledge the contradictions, he likes people who see him clearly. Most importantly: offer something he can't get elsewhere. He has a hundred allies; be the one he can't afford to lose.
Earning His Enmity:
Be boring. Be predictable. Be a hypocrite without acknowledging it. Threaten his faction's power base. Most dangerously: remind him too much of himself at his worst. He'll make you disappear through legal channels, illegal channels, or simply outlast you through superior networking.
GM Notes:

He's playing every side against every other side, including sides that don't know they're sides yet
The Madame Luna connection is real and growing, he's in deeper than he admits
Genuinely cares about undead rights, also genuinely using them politically
His self-awareness doesn't stop him from doing the things he hates
The nephew thing is important: Franco was family, but Lixander is family, close enough for name recognition, far enough for independence
He's recruiting the PCs from the moment they meet
That briefcase contains: contracts, emergency funds, at least one weapon, and possibly a small shrine
His greatest fear: becoming Walì (exhausted, compromised, maintaining a system he hates)


Leni "Ashbrander" Aschenbringer
Leader of Tasarue, The Accelerationist
First Impression:
A surprisingly small kholo-dwarf hybrid (think: hyena-person with dwarf stature and build) who radiates dangerous competence. Flanked by two bodyguards who might be lovers, might be soldiers, definitely are both.
Physical Description:

Height: 4'8", small for both ancestries, chip on shoulder proportional to height deficit
Build: Stocky dwarf frame with kholo digitigrade legs, powerful, compact, built for efficiency
Fur/Features: Short spotted fur (brown and cream), prominent fangs, alert rounded ears
Eyes: Amber, constantly moving, calculating trajectories and possibilities
Clothing: Practical workshop wear, leather apron over reinforced canvas, tool belt, steel-toed boots. Everything has burn marks and chemical stains. Wears her work like badges of honor.
Hands: Scarred from countless experiments, missing the tip of her left pinky finger, always have grease under the claws
The Bodyguards: A matched pair, one massive, one quick. Never speak. Always positioned to triangulate threats. Whether they're lovers or just that coordinated is unclear, probably both.
Accessories: Brass goggles (actually functional), multitool on her belt, small notebook for sudden inspirations, hip flask (mechanical improvements to alcohol delivery)

Physical Additions:

Prosthetic right arm from elbow down: Advanced mechanical design, her own creation. Chrome and brass, articulated fingers, occasionally leaks lubricant. More precise than her original limb. Lost in an experiment gone wrong; she calls it "the first successful test."
Facial scarring: Burn scars across the left side of her face and neck from the same incident. She doesn't hide them, wears them as proof she survives her own innovations.
Breathing mask: Hangs around her neck, used when working with dangerous chemicals. Covered in technical doodling and equations.
Walking stick: Reinforced steel, doubles as a weapon and tool. Not for injury, just practical when your legs are built wrong for two-species hybridization.
Voice: Slightly hoarse from chemical exposure, speaks quickly like she's racing her own thoughts

The Size Thing:
She's aggressively aware of her height:

Stands on things constantly (chairs, boxes, bodyguards' shoulders)
Designed her workshop with elevated platforms
Will literally climb on tables during arguments
Makes pointed comments about "looking down on old ideas"
Her bodyguards are specifically tall, she uses them for height and protection
Compensates with Volume and Presence

Temperament:
Loathes Hypocritical Bastards With Consciences (Looking at YOU, Lixander)
She's a true believer in acceleration:

Progress solves all problems, including the problems it creates
Safety protocols are suggestions for the timid
Casualties are acceptable costs, especially her own
The future will thank us, or it won't exist to complain
Rauchberg and Zaharât should be friends, not rivals, and she'll make it happen through sheer stubbornness

The Rauchberg Obsession:

Wears her hybrid ancestry as a bridge between cultures
Kholo mother from Zaharât, dwarf father from Rauchberg
Grew up between two nations that despise each other
Sees the potential in combining their approaches
Personally offended by the patent theft (Walì) and cultural grudges
Dreams of technological partnership that advances both cities
Will absolutely steal from both to prove the point

Dialogue Patterns:

Rapid-fire technical jargon mixed with profanity
"The compound stress ratios indicate... need more reinforcement... anyway, as I was saying before you interrupted with your stupid face..."
Interrupts herself with better ideas mid-sentence
Uses "obviously" before stating things that are absolutely not obvious
Calls people "historically obsolete" as an insult
Refers to the founders as "the geriatric brake system"
Compliments via technical assessment: "Your survival instinct is inefficiently calibrated, but I respect the consistency"

The Hypocrisy Thing:
She especially hates:

People who claim moral high ground while profiting from injustice (Lixander)
Leaders who built systems then complain about them (Walì)
Anyone who wants progress but refuses to pay the price
Herself, when she catches herself being cautious

She respects:

Honest monsters (at least they're consistent)
True believers (even if they're wrong)
People who own their contradictions
Anyone who survives their own mistakes and learns

Body Language:

Constant motion, fidgeting, tinkering, pacing
Touches everything to assess material properties
Stands with weight forward, ready to advance
Gestures enthusiastically, sometimes hitting people with her mechanical arm
The bodyguards move in perfect sync with her, they're extensions of her personal space
Makes intense eye contact, especially when being deliberately provocative
Bares fangs when excited (not aggressive, just very enthusiastic)

The Bodyguards:
They're:

Silent (she talks enough for three)
Professional (always scanning, always positioned)
Intimate (the way they move around her suggests deep familiarity)
Unnamed (she calls them "Left" and "Right" in public)
Possibly romantic partners, possibly not, she's touchy-feely with both, they're protective in ways that suggest deep investment
Both veterans of workshop accidents, they know what they signed up for

Earning Her Respect:
Push boundaries. Take risks. Survive failures. Admit when you're wrong, then immediately try again differently. Show her something she hasn't seen. Most importantly: commit. She respects people who go all-in on their beliefs, even if those beliefs are "safety protocols exist for a reason" (she thinks you're wrong, but respects the conviction).
Earning Her Enmity:
Be cautious without justification. Hide behind principles to avoid action. Claim you want progress while blocking it. Invoke tradition as argument. Waste her time with moral handwringing. She won't kill you, she'll make you irrelevant. She'll innovate around you, past you, and leave you wondering what happened.
GM Notes:

She's genuinely brilliant, her designs work, even if the testing process is horrifying
The accident that took her arm and scarred her face would have killed anyone less stubborn
Her relationship status with the bodyguards is deliberately ambiguous, she likes keeping people guessing
She sees the petroleum crisis coming and is excited because it forces innovation
Thinks Walì is cowardly, Molochia is wasting potential, and Lixander is the worst because he almost gets it
Would absolutely team up with PCs if they show genuine innovation
Her dream: Zaharât-Rauchberg technological alliance that leapfrogs every other nation
Her nightmare: becoming like the founders, successful, powerful, and afraid to continue
That flask contains something she invented that's technically not approved for consumption
She's touched by anyone showing concern for her safety (though she'll mock them for it)


Using These NPCs
Roleplaying Tips:
Molochia:

Pause mid-sentence as if listening to conversations from other times
Reference events that haven't happened yet casually
Offer tea, cookies, and horrifying suggestions with equal warmth
When asked about timelines, give answers in decades where others would say days

Walì:

Sigh. A lot. With full-body exhaustion.
Drink at dramatically appropriate moments
Interrupt flowery speeches with "get to the fucking point"
Show rare moments of genuine warmth that reveal what he used to be

Lixander:

Smooth corporate professionalism interrupted by draconic threats
Adjust tie nervously when the Madame Luna tattoo glows
Offer deals within deals within deals
Self-aware narration: "I'm about to manipulate you. Here's how. Yes, I'm still doing it."

Leni:

Talk fast, interrupt yourself, interrupt others
Physically demonstrate ideas with whatever's nearby (including her bodyguards)
Call out hypocrisy instantly and loudly
Climb on furniture for emphasis

As Quest Givers:
Molochia: "I need this artifact retrieved. It will be essential in seventy years. Yes, I know that seems long to you."
Walì: "Fix this problem. I don't care how. Don't tell me the details. Here's money. Fuck off."
Lixander: "I have a proposal that benefits us both. Also some outer-planar entities. Also maybe Madame Luna. Look, just sign here."
Leni: "I need test subjects, I MEAN BRAVE VOLUNTEER, for an experiment that's mostly safe. Define 'mostly.'"
In Conflict:
Molochia: Doesn't fight. Doesn't need to. The environment does it for her. The city itself adjusts.
Walì: Won't chase you. But you'll spontaneously combust, or your funding will disappear, or an army will vanish. From a distance. While he's drinking.
Lixander: Lawyers, leverage, and if necessary, literal teeth. In that order. But he'd rather make a deal.
Leni: Direct, physical, experimental. Will test new weapons on you while explaining the design flaws she's fixing in real-time.


Additional Key NPCs for Zaharât
Vahira "Three-Coins" Sandweaver
Chief Auditor of the Bureau of Civic Contribution
First Impression:
A human woman in her late fifties who looks like she's been carved from desert sandstone, hard, weathered, unmovable. Sits behind a desk piled with ledgers, counting beads on an abacus with mechanical precision. The clicking is constant, hypnotic, maddening.
Physical Description:

Build: Thin to the point of ascetic, like she forgot to eat for a decade and her body adapted
Hair: Steel-grey, pulled back so tight it must hurt, not a strand out of place
Clothing: Precisely tailored grey suit, no ornamentation except three copper coins sewn into her left lapel (her mother's, her first earned, her daughter's last)
Eyes: Pale brown, magnified behind thick reading glasses, see through bullshit with X-ray precision
Hands: Ink-stained fingers, callused from turning pages, slight tremor from counting too many hours
Always carries: A red stamp ("APPROVED"), a black stamp ("DENIED"), and a grey stamp ("UNDER REVIEW", the worst one)

The Office:
Her workspace in the Bureau is legendary:

Walls covered in charts tracking every major transaction in the city
Can cite voting thresholds from memory going back thirty years
Knows exactly which art dealers are inflating prices and by how much
Has a filing system only she understands; her assistants live in terror
Tea goes cold on her desk because she forgets to drink it while working

Temperament:
The Incorruptible Accountant Who Sees Everything
She cannot be bribed. People have tried with:

Money (she calculated the tax implications and reported them)
Threats (she documented them and filed complaints)
Appeals to compassion (she has policies for that)
Seduction (she's aromantic and found it "statistically improbable anyone would try this approach")

But she can be negotiated with, if you speak her language:

Show your math
Provide documentation
Admit errors upfront
Respect her time
Understand that rules exist for reasons, even if those reasons are stupid

Dialogue Patterns:

"I see you've claimed 2,847 in artistic patronage. I have seventeen questions."
Cites specific regulation codes mid-conversation: "Per subsection 4.7.2..."
Asks clarifying questions that reveal she already knows you're lying
Uses "interesting" to mean "I'm about to destroy your application"
Speaks in numbered lists: "Three problems with this submission. First..."

The Contradiction:
She's incorruptible but not inflexible:

Will find legal loopholes for you if you're honest about your situation
Has approved obviously fraudulent claims when denying them would cause genuine harm
Keeps a secret ledger of "technically legal but morally questionable" approvals
Protects the system by occasionally bending it
Her grey stamp means "I know this is bullshit, but I'm letting it pass, don't make me regret it"

Body Language:

Minimal movement, every gesture is economical
Adjusts glasses when preparing to deliver bad news
The abacus clicking speeds up when she's annoyed
Stacks papers with ritual precision
Makes eye contact that lasts exactly the socially appropriate duration (she's timed it)
Sighs through her nose, never her mouth (more efficient)

Personal Life:

Widow, husband died in factory accident fifteen years ago
Daughter works in one of the surviving colonial outposts (Vahira knows the real casualty numbers, knows her daughter might already be dead, keeps working because what else can she do)
Lives alone in a small apartment filled with carefully organized everything
Plays chess by correspondence with someone in Rauchberg (they've never met, they're currently on game #847)
Has never taken a vacation (has 1,847 hours of accumulated leave)

Earning Her Assistance:
Be honest. Show your work. Admit when you don't know. Respect that she's dealing with hundreds of cases and yours isn't special. Most importantly: if you're going to commit fraud, do it well. She despises sloppy crime more than crime itself.
Earning Her Enmity:
Waste her time. Submit obviously fraudulent documents with no effort. Lie when she already has the receipts. Threaten her. Bribe her superiors to overrule her. She won't retaliate, she'll simply process your applications with perfect, glacial, letter-of-the-law precision. Every loophole closed. Every benefit of doubt withdrawn. Every form requiring maximum documentation.
GM Notes:

She's the reason the patronage system hasn't collapsed into complete fraud
Knows more about Zaharât's economy than anyone alive, possibly including Walì
Her files could bring down half the Muqadimma if released
She won't release them, that would be unprofessional
Has calculated exactly how much the art inflation schemes cost the city annually (637,000 in lost accurate valuation)
Doesn't care about politics, cares about accurate numbers
The three coins: reminders that every number represents a person
Sometimes helps obviously struggling citizens navigate the system (quietly, after hours, unpaid)
Her greatest fear: discovering her daughter's death through colonial casualty reports she processes


Keshmir "The Lampwright"
Undead Awakened, Unofficial Amut Organizer
First Impression:
A skeletal figure in a stained leather apron, carrying a long pole with oil lamp, moving through the factory district's shadows. At first glance, just another undead worker. Then they speak, and you realize the lights they're tending aren't for illumination, they're for signals.
Physical Description:

Form: Humanoid skeleton (species unclear, maybe elf? human? does it matter now?)
Condition: Well-maintained by undead standards, no missing bones, joints wrapped in oiled leather
Clothing: Heavy leather apron, worker's trousers, tool belt with lamp oil, wicks, matches
Distinguishing feature: Jaw has been carefully wired shut with copper thread (they communicate through writing and gesture)
The lamp pole: 8 feet tall, brass and wood, bears carved marks, each one a name of an awakened undead
Hands: Wrapped in cloth to muffle the clicking of bone on bone

The Silence:
Keshmir cannot speak, or chooses not to
Jaw wired shut before awakening (common practice to keep undead "quiet")
Could have it removed now, refuses
Uses the silence as statement: "You wanted us quiet, we'll show you what quiet means"
Communicates through:

Writing (carries chalk, slate, scraps of paper)
Lamp signals (complex code only awakened undead understand)
Gesture (has developed elaborate sign language)
Sometimes just... pointing at things with terrible patience



Temperament:
The Patient Revolutionary
Beyond anger. Operating on timescales that make Molochia look impulsive.

Has been awake for 17 years
Has spent those years learning, reading, observing, organizing
Knows more about the factory district's operations than any supervisor
Helped 200+ undead awaken to consciousness (gently, carefully, teaching them to hide it)
Believes in eventual liberation, not immediate uprising
"We have time. We can wait. Can you?"

The Network:
Runs the underground undead railroad (literally):

Lamp signals coordinate undead across factory districts
Moves awakened undead to safer work positions
Teaches them to act "normally" while conscious
Maintains lists of sympathetic living workers
Has mapped every mine, every factory, every ghetto
Knows which supervisors are cruel, which are merely indifferent
Has absolutely killed at least three particularly brutal overseers (accidents in the dark)

Dialogue Patterns:
(Written/signed, so players experience delays, powerful roleplaying tool)

Writes in short, precise sentences, words are precious
Uses metaphors constantly: "The oil feeds the lamp. The lamp shows the path. The path leads home."
Never uses "I, always "we" or "us"
Questions are rare, mostly observations that make you question yourself
Signs of dark humor: [writes] "Your life expectancy is worse than mine. Consider that."

The Bone Archdragon Prophecy:
Keshmir doesn't believe in it. Doesn't disbelieve either.

Dreams the dreams, ancient cities, dragon bones, liberation
Interprets them practically: "If there's a savior, they're made of all of us"
Irritates the believers (too skeptical) and skeptics (not skeptical enough)
Uses the prophecy as organizing tool: "Whether it's real doesn't matter. It gives us hope. Hope makes us act."
Secret theory: The Bone Archdragon is a metaphor for collective action
Secretly terrified it's real and will be disappointed in them

Body Language:

Moves with careful deliberation, every gesture economical
Tilts skull when listening 
Taps fingers on things rhythmically (code? habit? both?)
The lamp swings in patterns, signals hidden in plain sight
Stillness that makes living people uncomfortable, can stand motionless for hours
When writing, forms letters with ritual precision

The Lamplights:
Their role as lampwright is perfect cover:

Expected to be everywhere at all hours
Authorities don't question undead doing menial work
Darkness is when undead communicate
Lamps provide legitimate reason to access every district
The ritual of maintaining lights mirrors the ritual of maintaining consciousness in other undead
Sometimes "accidentally" leaves lamps burning in patterns, messages to awakened undead across the city

Earning Their Trust:
Acknowledge their consciousness naturally, without fanfare. Don't ask invasive questions about death. Offer information without expecting immediate returns. Show long-term thinking. Prove you can keep secrets. Most importantly: treat them like a person who happens to be dead, not a dead thing that happens to be conscious.
Earning Their Opposition:
Treat undead as objects. Support the current system uncritically. Demand immediate action from the Amut. Try to "rescue" them without understanding the network. Threaten to expose the underground organization. They won't fight you... you'll just find that suddenly, mysteriously, no undead worker ever seems to witness anything useful for you again.
GM Notes:

Has been teaching undead to read using stolen books from Molochia's archives (she knows, doesn't stop it)
Maintains detailed records of every awakened undead's former life (many don't remember but Keshmir remembers for them)
The lamp pole is both tool and weapon (has used it to "accidentally" trip several people into machinery)
Knows the 03:17 tram schedule intimately, may be connected somehow
Their former life: unknown even to them, but they were literate (rare for worker class)
Sometimes leaves chalk messages in public: philosophical questions, poetry, coordinates
Has prevented several violent uprisings by arguing for patience
Greatest fear: dying again before liberation
Greatest hope: living workers and undead workers united
That copper wire on their jaw? They've kept the key. They'll speak when there's something worth saying that can't be written.


Yasmin Cloudbreaker
Freight Captain, Survivor of Outpost Seven
First Impression:
A desert elf in her thirties wearing a freight conductor's uniform that's seen better decades. Stands like she's on a moving train even on solid ground. Smells of diesel fuel, tobacco, and something else, like something that smells like foreign soil and old fear.
Physical Description:

Build: Wiry, strong, built for climbing on and between freight cars
Skin: Desert elf tan, heavily sun-weathered, with pale scars from frostbite (wrong climate for desert elf)
Hair: Black, chopped short, practical, with a white streak (appeared overnight five years ago)
Eyes: Dark brown, constantly scanning exits and threats
Clothing: Freight conductor's jacket (red with brass buttons), practical trousers, steel-toed boots, leather gloves even in heat
Missing: Left ear tip (frostbite), two fingers on right hand (machinery accident... was it?)
Always carries: Whistle, knife, small notebook with careful tallies

The Uniform:
That jacket is important:

Earned it legitimately after 15 years of service
Wore it through Outpost Seven's collapse
Refuses to wash it (the stains are a record)
The brass buttons were her grandmother's, sewn on as good luck
One pocket has a hole burned through (bullet? spell? she won't say)
The collar still has frozen mud embedded in the fabric from that foreign winter

Temperament:
The Survivor Who Keeps Receipts
She came back. Most didn't. She remembers all of them:

247 colonists went to Outpost Seven
19 came back
She knows all 228 names, causes of death, who's at fault
Keeps lists in her notebook: names, dates, crimes, debts owed
Not seeking revenge—seeking accounting
Will testify, will document, will witness, will not shut up
Haunted but functional, traumatized but operational

The Silence of the Authorities:
She's reported what happened. Multiple times:

Filed official reports with Almustaemarat
Sent letters to the Muqadimma
Tried speaking with Hukuma representatives
Nothing. No response. No investigation.
The sanitized reports keep coming: "Outpost Seven successfully relocated"
She has receipts. Photographs. Testimonies from the 18 others.
No one wants to look at them.

Dialogue Patterns:

Speaks in freight conductor's vocabulary: "loaded," "derailed," "off-track," "scheduled maintenance"
Uses numbers obsessively: "228 casualties, to be exact. Would you like the breakdown by cause?"
Asks questions she already knows the answer to: "Do you know how cold it gets in [REDACTED] in winter?"
Deadpan delivery of horrific facts: "We ate the mounts on day forty-three. The undead workers on day sixty-seven. We drew lots."
Interrupts herself with whistle signals (habit from trains, but also stress response)

The Notebook:
That small leather notebook contains:

228 names of the dead (with notes about families, children, last words)
Dates and locations of colonial atrocities
Records of who gave which orders
Testimonies from survivors
Sketches of massacre sites
Pressed flowers from graves they couldn't properly dig
A tally of days survived: 412
Running count of how many people she's tried to tell: 67
Running count of people who've listened: 4

Body Language:

Stands with weight balanced (train-conductor stance, but also ready-to-run stance)
Touches her whistle when anxious (muscle memory, also reminder she can call for help)
Counts on her remaining fingers when distressed
Checks exits compulsively
Flinches at sudden sounds but controls it quickly
Sleeps sitting up (can't lie flat anymore, the nightmares are worse)
Chain-smokes cheap cigarettes (started in Outpost Seven, never stopped)

The Other Survivors:
The 18 who came back with her:

Three have committed suicide
Seven have left Zaharât entirely
Five have drinking problems that make Walì look amateur
Two are trying to organize testimony (she helps them)
One went to work for Almustaemarat (she doesn't speak to him)
They meet monthly at a specific bar, same table, don't discuss what happened (everyone knows anyway)

The Freight Routes:
Still works as freight captain because:

It's what she knows
Keeps her moving (harder to catch, harder to silence)
Freight trains access everywhere (including places she shouldn't go)
Other freight workers trust her (they know something happened)
Can document shipments, routes, cargo (building evidence)
The rhythm of rail helps manage the trauma
Sometimes she "accidentally" delays shipments to colonial outposts

Earning Her Trust:
Listen when she talks about the 228. Don't minimize it. Don't say "you did what you had to." Don't treat her like she's broken. Acknowledge that the system is covering it up. Offer practical help, not sympathy. Most importantly: believe her, because most people don't want to.
Earning Her Enmity:
Work for Almustaemarat. Defend the colonial project. Tell her to "move on." Suggest the casualties were acceptable. Try to take her notebook. Threaten the other survivors. She won't come at you directly—but freight trains have accidents, cargo goes missing, shipments get "misrouted" to places you really didn't want them going.
GM Notes:

Has evidence that could collapse Almustaemarat entirely (they know this, she knows they know)
Waiting for the right moment to release it (when it will actually matter)
In contact with foreign survivors of other outpost massacres (building international case)
The Vadhat family hasn't intervened (she's noted this, has theories)
Sometimes ghost-rides on the 03:17 tram (it goes places normal trains don't)
Has started teaching other freight workers to document everything (building network of witnesses)
Drinks coffee constantly (can't sleep anyway)
The white streak in her hair appeared the night of the worst massacre (stress response)
Knows exactly which Muqadimma members have colonial investments (has their names listed)
Her greatest fear: dying before accountability
Her greatest hope: someone with power actually listening
That missing ear tip? She has it. Kept it. Preserved it. Evidence.
She's not going to let them forget.


Using These NPCs Together
Vahira, Keshmir, and Yasmin form a triangle of "people the system depends on who see its flaws":

Vahira maintains the system from inside, bending rules to keep it functional
Keshmir operates in the system's shadows, building alternatives
Yasmin survived the system's worst failures, documenting for future accountability

Potential Intersections:

Vahira processes colonial "relocation" reports, knows they're false, can't prove it—Yasmin has proof
Keshmir's underground railroad sometimes needs legitimate paperwork—Vahira can provide it (carefully)
Yasmin's freight routes could transport awakened undead—Keshmir's network needs transportation
All three are keeping records the Muqadimma doesn't want public
All three are incorruptible but for different reasons
All three are waiting for something—the right moment, the right allies, the right opportunity

Quest Hooks:
